# Vice City Ledger — GTA VI Trophy Guide & Tracker

[![make.fun](https://img.shields.io/badge/make.fun-gta6--trophy--guide--01-orange)](https://make.fun/c/gta6-trophy-guide-01)
[![License: MIT](https://img.shields.io/badge/License-MIT-yellow.svg)](LICENSE)
[![Trophies](https://img.shields.io/badge/trophies-55%2F55-blue)](app/data/trophies.json)

An offline guide and tracker for all **55 GTA VI trophies** on PS5: the
official names, descriptions and grades, a checklist that saves in the
browser (with export and import), filters by kind and grade, a spoiler
shield for hidden story trophies, per-trophy count trackers, a 4-stage
roadmap to the platinum, the one possibly-missable trophy flagged with both
sides of the dispute, and a guide for every trophy in our own words — each
step citing its source.

**Bounty:** [gta6-trophy-guide-01 on make.fun](https://make.fun/c/gta6-trophy-guide-01) · **Run it:** serve `app/` with any static server and open `index.html`. No build step, no network at runtime.
