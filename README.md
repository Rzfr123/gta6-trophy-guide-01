# Vice City Ledger — GTA VI Trophy Guide & Tracker

[![make.fun](https://img.shields.io/badge/make.fun-gta6--trophy--guide--01-orange)](https://make.fun/c/gta6-trophy-guide-01)
[![License: MIT](https://img.shields.io/badge/License-MIT-yellow.svg)](LICENSE)
[![Trophies](https://img.shields.io/badge/trophies-55%2F55-blue)](app/data/trophies.json)

Offline guide & tracker for all **55 GTA VI trophies** (PS5): official names,
descriptions and grades, a checklist saved in the browser with export/import,
kind + grade filters, a spoiler shield, per-trophy count trackers, a 4-stage
roadmap to the platinum, the one possibly-missable trophy flagged, and a guide
for every trophy in our own words — every step cited.

**Bounty:** [gta6-trophy-guide-01 on make.fun](https://make.fun/c/gta6-trophy-guide-01) · **Run it:** serve `app/` with any static server, open `index.html`. No build step, no network.
